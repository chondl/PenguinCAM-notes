Scenario strip: mockup controls, not part of the product Decisions to react to Layout step, options to compare:

How to use this mockup. The dark panel on the right is the wizard; go through it with Next. On its Parts step, click parts in the list or the picture on the left, as you would in Onshape. Nothing is real: no slicing, no Onshape, no printer. To compare this round: on the Layout step, select a copy, then switch Lay on face, Mouse and Parts arrive in this yellow strip; each works at once on the plate in front of you. Open Advanced mode from the Setup step at each Panel width, or drag the panel's left edge (the real panel can probably be dragged wider in Onshape; that is not confirmed). The strip also makes an error happen (choose one, press Arm, carry on) and switches to an Assembly or the standalone page. It is not part of the product.
